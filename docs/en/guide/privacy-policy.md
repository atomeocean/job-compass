# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 27, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit Job Compass at jobcompass.atomeocean.com (the "Site"). The Site is operated by Atomeocean ("we", "us"), a technology company based in New York, NY, United States.

## Summary

- You can read everything on the Site without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details.
- The Site uses third-party analytics and advertising services. They collect information about your visit, such as the pages you view and your approximate location, and some of them use cookies.
- Contributions to Job Compass are made through GitHub and are public.

## Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, your browser sends technical information to the services below, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the referring page, the pages you view and the time of your visit.

| Service | Provider | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Analytics | Google LLC | Measures how visitors use the Site. Uses cookies. |
| Google AdSense | Google LLC | Shows advertising. Uses cookies; see [Advertising](#advertising). |
| Cloudflare Web Analytics | Cloudflare, Inc. | Measures traffic and page performance without cookies. |
| Umami | Umami (cloud.umami.is) | Counts page views without cookies. |
| Busuanzi | Busuanzi (busuanzi.ibruce.info) | Counts the page views and visitors shown on the Site. May use a cookie to recognize returning visitors. |
| GitHub Pages | GitHub, Inc. | Hosts the Site. May log requests, including IP addresses. |

We do not run our own database for the Site and do not store visitor information ourselves. Information collected by these services is held by the providers under their own privacy policies.

## Advertising

The Site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Comments

Some pages have a comment section powered by Giscus, which stores comments in GitHub Discussions. To comment, you sign in with your GitHub account. Your comment, username and profile picture are public, and GitHub's [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies.

## Contributions

Job Compass is developed in a [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/atomeocean/job-compass). If you contribute, for example by opening a pull request or an issue, your GitHub username, profile picture and what you submit, including commit details, become publicly visible.

## Interview reports and personal information

Interview reports describe hiring processes, not individuals. We do not knowingly publish the real names or contact details of candidates or interviewers. If a page contains information about you that you would like removed, contact us and we will review the request.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. To stop Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits, you can install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
