# Interview Experience Contribution Guide

## Contribution Principles

1. **Authenticity**: Make sure the interview experience you share is your own or has been confirmed to be genuine.
2. **Privacy**:
   - Do not disclose the author's real name, contact details, or other non-public personal information.
3. **Objectivity and courtesy**: Describe the interview objectively and avoid hostile language.
4. **No filler content**: AI tools may only be used to help generate the JSON file.
5. **Source quality**: A reposted interview experience must include at least enough question or process detail to be restated. The following are not accepted:
   - Posts whose core content is only a link to a commercial website, question bank, or course
   - Posts from accounts used mainly to drive traffic or promote something
   - Posts that are obviously fabricated or duplicate existing content
   - LLM-generated posts

   Published content later found to fall into the first three categories is moved to `folded-entry/`. LLM-generated content is deleted.

## Before You Start

1. This is a **brief guide** that only outlines the basic process and rules. For the full steps, see the [Atomeocean Ceremonial Contribution Process Guide](https://logbook.atomeocean.com/guide/light-weight-work/ceremonial-contribution/process-guide.html).
2. **Device requirements**: Use a laptop or desktop computer to complete the contribution process. Mobile devices such as iPads and iPhones are not supported.
3. **Account requirements**: You need to register and sign in to a [GitHub](https://github.com/) account.

Contributions of interview experiences to Job Compass are welcome. What you share helps other job seekers prepare for their interviews. Please follow the rules and process below.

## Basic Steps

1. **Prepare the content**: Organize the basic interview information (JSON file) and the interview details (Markdown article), and cite your sources.
2. **Submit a PR**: Submit a Pull Request to the Job Compass project on GitHub. Each PR should contain only one interview experience.
3. **Wait for review**: The PR must pass the automated build check and be manually reviewed and confirmed by at least one community member.
4. **Merge and publish**: Once approved, the PR is merged into the main branch, and after a successful deployment the article is published automatically to the site's interview experience pages.
