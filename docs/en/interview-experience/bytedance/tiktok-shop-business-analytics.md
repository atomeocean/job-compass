### Business Analytics Case: Contact per Order

One scenario involved Contact per Order, a customer service metric measuring the number of customer support contacts relative to the number of orders.

The question was: If Contact per Order suddenly increases over a short period, how would you investigate?

My approach was to:

1. Verify the metric definition and data quality to confirm the increase was genuine.
2. Break down the metric by region, time period, order type, and customer issue category.
3. Compare affected segments against historical patterns to identify where the increase originated.
4. Investigate potential causes, such as delivery delays, product quality concerns, returns and refunds, or platform process changes.
5. Prioritize the key drivers, recommend improvements, and monitor performance afterward.

### Business Research Case: Evaluating Graphic Design Agencies

Another scenario involved identifying graphic design agencies that could meet specific business needs. The task was to conduct initial research, screen potential firms, contact suitable candidates, and present two or three recommendations to the team.

My approach was to:

1. Clarify business requirements, deliverables, budget, and timeline.
2. Research potential agencies through publicly available information.
3. Establish screening criteria, including relevant industry experience, portfolio quality, service capabilities, client references, and budget fit.
4. Compare shortlisted agencies and validate their ability to deliver.
5. Recommend two or three candidates, explaining their strengths, potential risks, and overall suitability.

### Interview Preparation Advice

This role emphasizes not only technical analytics skills but also business understanding and the ability to translate insights into operational improvements.

I recommend reviewing e-commerce business processes, customer experience metrics, anomaly investigation frameworks, and practical examples of data automation and cross-functional collaboration.

For case questions, start by clarifying the business problem, then explain a structured analytical approach before discussing potential recommendations.
