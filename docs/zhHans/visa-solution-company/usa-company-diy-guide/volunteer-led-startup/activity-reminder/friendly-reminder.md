# Atomeocean轻量工作友好提醒

向轻量工作组发送邮件提醒，定期提醒关注活跃度


## 邮件通知

目前还是手动触发信息

### 邮件参与者信息

收件的订阅列表
```text
轻量工作组订阅邮件列表
```

发件人
```text
HR邮箱
```

抄送人
```text
管理层邮箱
```

邮件回复至
```text
HR邮箱
```

### 邮件内容信息

邮件主题
```text
[Atomeocean] Friendly Reminder: Stay Active and Engaged in the Lightweight Team
```

邮件正文
```text
Hi Atomeocean team members,

We hope you’re doing well!

As a member of the Atomeocean Lightweight Work Team, we kindly remind you to consider your ongoing compliance and participation level within the GitHub organization. Staying consistently active is important for keeping your lightweight work status valid and in good standing.

To help you review your current activity status, we’ve prepared this page

https://logbook.atomeocean.com/team-information/light-weight-team-information/light-weight-team-contributions

If you’re unsure how to increase your contributions or have questions about what qualifies as valid activity, we 
encourage you to join the job-compass-discussion channel in our Discord server. Our team is always happy to provide 
suggestions and support.

Join Atomeocean Discord: https://logbook.atomeocean.com/about-us/discord-tutorial

Thanks for being part of the team, and we look forward to seeing your continued contributions!

Best regards,

The Atomeocean Team
```

## Discord通知


Discord对私聊支持较差，只能通过oceaner app在频道通知同时艾特用户id。建议

```text
[轻量工作组] 麻烦帮我看下这个 PR～ 
PR链接: 点击查看PR
感谢配合！
@userId
```
