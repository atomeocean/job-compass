# Atomeocean轻量工作紧急提醒

当轻量员工活跃度过低时，HR会发送紧急提醒邮件，告知员工活跃度过低的情况，并要求其在规定时间内进行改善。

会发送邮件信息和微信消息，并考虑在discord里面发信息。


## 邮件通知

### 邮件参与者信息

收件人
```text
hr手动查看低活跃度员工
```

发件人
```text
HR邮箱
```

抄送人
```text
管理层邮箱
```

邮件回复至
```text
HR邮箱
```

### 邮件内容信息

邮件主题，后面添加当前日期 2025-05-07
```text
[Atomeocean]Urgent Reminder: Action Required for Your Lightweight Work Status
```

邮件正文
```text
Hi Team Member,

We hope you're well.

We've previously reached out to remind you about the importance of maintaining consistent activity within our Atomeocean Lightweight Work Team. However, we've noticed that your participation level remains very low, with minimal or no recorded contributions so far.

It's crucial to understand that sustained inactivity could affect your lightweight work status and eligibility within our organization. To quickly address this issue, please review your current activity status here:

https://logbook.atomeocean.com/team-information/light-weight-team-information/light-weight-team-contributions

If you're facing challenges or have questions about how to effectively increase your activity level, we strongly encourage you to connect with us directly on Discord job-compass-discussion.

Your prompt action on this matter is appreciated. Thank you for your immediate attention and understanding.

Best regards,
```

## 微信消息

```text
您好，公司发了一封邮件，主题是[Atomeocean]Urgent Reminder: Action Required for Your Lightweight Work Status。请尽快提高活跃度，保持工作状态。
```

更人性化一些的
```text
您好，公司需要您提高活跃度保持合规工作状态。请查看这篇快速拯救你的开源活跃度 https://logbook.atomeocean.com/guide/light-weight-work/offboarding/how-to-regain-activity
```

提醒发送了一封邮件，如果一周内没有回复就执行最后一次提醒。
